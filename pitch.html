Agents in Context Toronto · Track 1: Agents You Can Audit

An ad budget agent an agency can actually trust

It moves client ad money across Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok and Reddit. Every action is tied to a person, checked against rules stored in MongoDB Atlas before it runs, verified afterwards, and recorded so anyone can replay why it happened.

Why I built it

I do marketing for a coffee company. This morning I reviewed an AI ad tool that changes budgets every 15 minutes and won't explain how it decides. Agencies can't hand client money to something like that. One bad night can cost thousands and lose the account.

The three Track 1 questions

Enforced before the action?Policies are documents with schema validation. They're checked when the agent asks and again at the moment an approved change runs.
What, why, under whose authority?Every policy check, approval (with a required comment), send and verified result lands in the audit trail. Explain rebuilds the full story of any change.
Stop or roll back mid run?A kill switch checked before every write, a circuit breaker that halts a client on its own when spend runs away, and one click undo through the same gate.

What it does for an agency

Staff approvals with limitsOwner approves anything, senior strategist up to $25 a day, junior can't approve. Clients only see reports.
Evidence on every decisionConfidence vs target, CPA range, baselines, trend, expected weekly impact, risk, learning phase effect.
Ad law built inHousing age targeting is denied for everyone. Health ads go to compliance review. Risky ad copy is blocked.
Memory that changes behaviorPast moves and results per client. When similar moves backfired, the agent asks a human instead.
Beyond adsAI search visibility (AEO/GEO), competitor ads, SEO vs paid overlap, review replies, tracking health.
Optimization skillsNext dollar returns, goal planner, stock-aware ads, search term mining, funnel leaks, experiments, live site audit.
Tracking that fixes itselfUTM links checked against one naming rule and fixed automatically. Conversions API, deduplication and click ID health per platform.
Retargeting ladderVisitors, viewers, carts, checkouts and past buyers each get their own window, message and budget. Overexposed tiers are cut after approval, buyer exclusions stay synced.
Rules in plain EnglishType "never spend more than $150 a day on dental clients". See exactly what it will do. Only the owner can switch it on, and every version is kept.
Time machineDrag to any past moment and see what the agent knew: rules in force, budgets, what was waiting, the kill switch.
Client report in plain words"Your ads cost $5,213 and brought 142 orders at $36.72 each, better than last week."
Platform truth checkDecisions use store or CRM verified conversions, not what Meta or TikTok claim.
